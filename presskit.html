About the game

Off Grid'n is a cozy, chaotic, low-poly road trip sim. It starts on your last Monday at CloudHog's call center: one server-room fire, one very awkward CEO and a $5,000 "please don't post about this" check later, you're free.

Down at Rusty's Used Rigs you pick a retired school bus or a beat-up van, rip out the seats and build it back into a home, part by part: solar panels on the roof, a charge controller, a battery, a fuse box, lights, a water tank, a pump, a sink, a bed. The power and water really work, and a friendly Wiring Guide walks you through every connection.

Then you hit the road across a mini USA (the Rockies, the Pacific coast, the desert, the plains and the bayou) and make money any way you can: courier gigs, pizza runs, fishing, hunting, flipping junk, growing food, mining Bitcoin. Buy land, build a homestead, hire farmhands and grow it into a ranch. Along the way a tech giant is draining the town's water, a "safety" company is reading every license plate in the county, and the local burger joint has a secret ingredient nobody should ask about.

Features

Build your rig

  • School buses, a short bus, cargo and high-roof vans
  • Rip out the seats, then place every part on a grid: beds, bunks, kitchens, storage, a wood stove
  • Paint and body shop (flames are extra), wraps and skins
  • Your rig dents where it hits things

Power & water that really work

  • Solar panels, MPPT charge controllers, lithium batteries, fuse boxes, inverters, an alternator tap
  • Fresh water tanks, pumps, sinks and showers, piped by hand
  • A Wiring Guide that always knows your next step
  • Weather and nightfall: your battery has to last

A mini USA

  • Five regions with their own towns, locals and jobs
  • Bison, gators, elk, mountain goats, coyotes and (maybe) Bigfoot
  • Landmarks: a fire lookout, a drive-in, an airstrip, hot springs, a lighthouse, an old quarry
  • Day and night, storms, wind and rain

Hustle for cash

  • Courier gigs, pizza deliveries and customer orders
  • Fishing, hunting (mind the wind), foraging, gold panning, metal detecting
  • Grow mushrooms, veggies and hydroponic towers
  • Flip junk on the Marketplace app, mine Bitcoin, hustle darts and cards at the bar

Grow an empire

  • Buy land and build a homestead (with a bunker underneath)
  • Farmhands who plant and pick, a packing shed, a tractor, a deep well and a water tower
  • A mine shaft and a rock crusher
  • Weekly contracts with the towns and a Business app to run it all

Story & trouble

  • Jobs across the country, and a stealth heist on CloudHog's server hall: keycards, hacking, sweeping cameras, guards
  • GTA-style trouble: ragdolls, witnesses who call 911, the sheriff, paramedics and the fire department
  • Get caught and you're booked: pay bail, sleep it off, or hack the cell lock and break out
  • Exploding oil drums, because of course

Keep playing

  • Skills from 1 to 99 with perks, a collection log, rare finds and pets
  • Crates, skins and a Swap Meet
  • Sandbox mode, and a Zombies mode where your bus is your fortress
  • Halloween corn maze, jack-o'-lanterns and more

Friends & comfort

  • Online co-op for up to 4 players
  • Photo mode with RigTok (see below)
  • Read-at-your-own-pace dialogue, or timed text
  • A cartoon gore switch for younger players

The parodies

Off Grid'n pokes fun at big tech, surveillance, fast food and gambling apps. Here's who you'll meet.

Big tech

CloudHog

"We own the cloud!" CloudHog is the tech giant you used to answer phones for. Its CEO, Chad Hoggins, livestreams surprise office visits; your boss Brent needs those 212 sheets by lunch (also Saturday, also Sunday); the wall says HUSTLE. SYNERGY. HYDRATE.

Then CloudHog builds a data center outside Dead Pine, and its cooling pumps drain the valley's water until every well runs dry. Its insurance check for the call center fire is blown up big on a billboard. You blow up something else.

Kid. You're the only one here who didn't scream. Here's five grand. Don't post about this.
Surveillance

Stalk Safety

On day two, "safety" cameras go up on every road into town. They read every license plate that goes by, and the sheriff can't stop bragging about them.

Hack one of their roadside boxes and the Stalk app shows what they really keep: every plate, every phone, every Bluetooth gadget, kept forever. The officer portal's password is admin. The search log shows who's been looking up whom, and the reason box says "(none)". Show the town and they march on the Sheriff's Department. After that, the cameras can come down however you like.

Nothing to hide, nothing to fear.
Fast food

McGrubbin's

"i'm grubbin' it!" The burger joint on the loop road sells burgers, fries, nuggets and shakes, plus a Grubbin' Meal that fills you right up. Say hi to Grubby.

Round the back, Mort has a cost-cutting secret ingredient and a very large meat grinder. He's got some jobs for you. You won't love them. The health inspector is on his way.

Beef costs a fortune. Mort has ideas.
Gambling apps

HogBet

CloudHog's slick crypto casino, with your old boss Brent as your VIP host. Crash, Dice, Limbo, Mines and Plinko, a live bets feed, a "provably fair" page, VIP ranks and rakeback, and a stats page that's usually in the red (that's the house edge). There's a Take a Break button, and you're encouraged to use it.

It's in-game money only, and the joke is on the casino.

We own the cloud. Now we own your weekends.
Social media

RigTok

Press P and the camera comes out. Every photo gets scored on wildlife, weird stuff, your rig, your dog and the locals. There's a trend of the day worth double likes, and likes pay out through the Tip Jar. Grow your followers, earn titles, and become the internet's favorite bus person.

Like, follow, and gut a school bus.
Crime & punishment

The County Sheriff's Department

Get caught and the sheriff takes your loot and books you into cell 1. Call the front desk and pay bail, sleep it off on the cot (they let you out at 8 AM), or hack the lock and run for it. Every breakout doubles your next bail.

Quit fiddling with that lock. I can hear you, you know.

About SoloDolo Games
