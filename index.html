The trailer

Five grand. No job. No plan. Perfect.

It's your last Monday at CloudHog's call center. One server-room fire, one very awkward CEO and $5,000 later, you're free.

Head down to Rusty's Used Rigs, pick a school bus or a van, rip the old seats out and turn it into a home: solar on the roof, a battery, a fuse box, running water, a bed. Then take it across a mini USA and hustle your way up, one gig at a time.

What you get up to

Gut it. Build it.

Rip out the old seats, then build it back your way: beds, kitchens, storage, a wood stove. Paint it, wrap it, give it flames.

Wire it for real

Solar panels, charge controllers, batteries, fuse boxes, pumps and tanks. Power and water really work, and a Wiring Guide shows you every step.

A mini USA to roam

The Rockies, the coast, the desert, the plains and the bayou, each with its own town and locals. Bison, gators, hot springs and a lighthouse or two.

Hustle for cash

Courier gigs, pizza runs, fishing, hunting, flipping junk, growing mushrooms, even mining Bitcoin. Every hustle pays differently.

Grow an empire

Buy land, build a homestead, hire farmhands, run a tractor and sign contracts with the towns. From one old bus to a whole ranch.

Take down CloudHog

A tech giant is after the town's water. Dig up proof across the country, then pull off the heist. (And mind the sheriff.)

Zombies mode

The world ended on a Tuesday. Scavenge and build by day, and survive the nights in a rig you made yourself.

Bring your friends

Co-op for up to four players. Build a rig together, or roll out as a convoy. Your dog's coming too.
